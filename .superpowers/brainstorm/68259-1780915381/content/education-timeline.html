C — Two-Column Swiss Grid

Years on the left, details on the right, separated by a gold vertical rule. Quiet and editorial — lets the content breathe. Works best for readers who scan vertically.

My recommendation: A — Vertical Timeline. It's the format academic hiring committees instantly recognise from CVs. The concurrent degree callout makes the 2020–2024 double-degree achievement immediately visible without explanation.
